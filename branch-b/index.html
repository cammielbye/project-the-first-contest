Rules for this test
  • Both players use the same 24 cards. Best of one.
  • Whole turns back and forth, 9 each. Turn 10 is shared: both players lock in, then reveal together.
  • Each turn: play up to one landscape and use up to one force. Using a force flips the card; its front pips stop counting and its back pips count instead.
  • A landscape can't use its force the turn it's played (Claude's reading). Cards that say "Next turn, flip this back" do so on their own at the start of their owner's turn.
  • Worlds hold up to 2×2. A world point is one of each element or four of one element, each pip counted once, as many as a world holds.
  • A world worth 3 world points is a perfect world and wins at once. Otherwise most world points after turn 10 wins.
  • Forces' effects are applied by hand with the buttons: flip, flip back, move, swap, return, extra play.
