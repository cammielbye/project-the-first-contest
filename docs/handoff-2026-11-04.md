## How Cam likes to work

- One step at a time. When there's no direct question, say where things stand and propose the next step.
- Log every idea in the design doc as Decided, Exploring or Parked. Mark it Decided only when Cam says so.
  Keep Cam's words separate from "Claude's notes, not adopted".
- Work only from the most recent card pool pass.
- Keep him honest about mechanics that are fun for one player but miserable for the other (CORE-07).
- Fun and elegance come first. Don't overbuild. Keep the design doc and the project files current.
- Card rules:
  - no "The" in card names (CARD-22);
  - no metal (LORE-11);
  - rules text under 8 words (CARD-23) and within 2 lines on the card;
  - no "hand" or "deal";
  - "take" is reserved for the ante.
- Don't call NPCs "rivals" yet. For now they're effectively random people in the world, with no shared history with the player.
- "Overworld" means the region and world maps, as distinct from town maps.

## Where things stand

### Overworld and the supercontinent (LORE-12, LORE-13, WORLD-01, WORLD-04, WORLD-12)

- **The world's story:** the 100 originals made the world (LORE-12). Today's continents broke off a supercontinent (LORE-13). A plot idea: find an old map of the supercontinent and work out where its places are on today's continents.
- **Rules for the map:**
  - coasts mustn't be obvious matching pairs;
  - each continent keeps 72% of its old crust dry, the real-world ratio;
  - a coast that's eroding fast can be a clue to why two coasts don't match.
- **Cam's taste:**
  - nothing that looks constructed (it feels uncanny);
  - only a handful of lakes;
  - sketch #60's ranges and coastline feel right;
  - a large overworld;
  - sailing like Phantom Hourglass and Golden Sun, not like FireRed's water travel.
- **Draft K (latest),** on the World Sketch board "The supercontinent, Draft K":
  - three pieces in a chain: the middle one shares a rift with each outer piece, and the two outer pieces touch only briefly;
  - sizes are about 56%, 15% and 27% of the old land;
  - the right continent is turned 90° clockwise;
  - its lake moved to the bottom of the middle island;
  - the left continent's side facing the middle island is a straight run of eroding cliffs, with sea stacks offshore;
  - three ridge islands, one holding the earth seat;
  - pale dotted shelves show the lost crust (Cam loves these).
- **Waiting on:** Cam's reaction to Draft K.
- **Proposed next step:** place towns and the main sea routes on the chosen draft. Test whether three continents give enough room to hide 100 originals.
- **To regenerate Draft K:** `python3 tools/overworld/pangaea_draft_K.py 321`.

### Cards

- **Current pass:** the third pass, 101 cards (`data/card-pool.md`, the Card Anatomy boards titled "Third pass"). Test rule: making a world that scores 3 world points wins at once.
- **Pangaea's force is World Quake:** "Every permanence becomes its own world." Jet Stream (Trade Winds) still reads the same, so **Trade Winds needs a new force.**
- **CARD-26 (Exploring), Diablo II rarity:**
  - Lower rarities do one thing as hard as anything in the pool. Higher rarities do more things, each less hard.
  - Every clause has a weight (from turning a card for the round up to removing it) and a reach (from one card with one affinity up to everything).
  - Commons would be heavy and narrow, rares wide and light. Pinnacles stay exempt, like Diablo's uniques.
  - This would reverse CARD-24 (Decided) and Option A.
  - Claude suggested a small test: force faces only, four peak commons, and pinnacles that ignore common forces.
  - No cards have been changed.
- **Tabled:**
  - Do originals persist between rounds?
  - How do the commons that return to the binder at round end (Fumarole, Spring, Eyrie, Grove) work with originals?
  - Cam is second-guessing persistence, because using an original's force face feels no different from using a copy's.
- **Where originals are found:**
  - most are spread at random;
  - a few themed spots hold mid-strength cards;
  - pinnacles sit in predictable places behind very hard contests;
  - maybe one of the four elemental pinnacles is found by exploring or a quest.
  - The Gap and Origin wait for later.
